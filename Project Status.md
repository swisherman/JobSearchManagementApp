## Required Criteria

An opportunity must normally satisfy all of the following:

- Remote only
- Part-time employee or contract engagement
- Hands-on individual-contributor work
- No Secret clearance
- No other government security-clearance requirement
- Reasonable alignment with Robert's experience
- Compatible with Robert's retirement transition

A role that violates one of these requirements should not be presented as a strong match merely because the technology fits.

## Preferred Technical Fit

Strong matches may involve:

- C#
- .NET
- ASP.NET Core
- Blazor
- MongoDB
- REST APIs
- Workflow automation
- Business-process automation
- PowerShell
- Docker
- Testing
- Application integration
- Internal tools
- Photoshop or creative-workflow automation

A role does not need every technology to qualify.

## Preferred Work Characteristics

Robert prefers work that is:

- Practical and hands-on
- Clearly scoped
- Individual-contributor focused
- Flexible in scheduling
- Remote
- Suitable for an experienced developer
- Connected to real business workflows
- Compatible with part-time availability
- Reasonable to sustain during retirement

## Exclusions

Exclude roles that:

- Require onsite or hybrid attendance.
- Require relocation.
- Require a Secret, Top Secret, or similar clearance.
- Are full-time-only when no flexible arrangement is stated.
- Are primarily people-management positions.
- Are commission-only sales positions.
- Are unpaid.
- Require substantial travel.
- Use “contract” to describe a full-time rigid schedule without flexibility.
- Have a poor relationship between requirements, compensation, and scope.

| Security clearance | Must not be required |
| Technical fit | Strong, Moderate, or Weak |
| Schedule fit | Strong, Moderate, or Weak |
| Experience fit | Strong, Moderate, or Weak |
| Portfolio evidence | Available or Gap |
| Compensation | Known, Unclear, or Unsuitable |
| Application effort | Low, Medium, or High |
| Overall decision | Apply, Save, Reject, or Investigate |
